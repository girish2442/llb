Unit I — General Principles of Crime

Conceptions of crime; crime, morality and other wrongs; actus reus and mens rea; variations in liability; parties to crime; BNS application, definitions and general explanations; gender neutrality; punishments and general exceptions (BNS Sections 1–44).

Unit II — Inchoate Crimes; Women and Children

Abetment (Sections 45–60), criminal conspiracy (Section 61), attempt (Section 62), sexual offences and deceitful means (Sections 63–73), criminal force and assault against women (Sections 74–79), selected marriage offences and offences against children (as specified in the syllabus).

Unit III — Offences Against the Human Body

Offences affecting life; culpable homicide and murder; death by rash or negligent act; suicide; organised crime and terrorist act; hurt and grievous hurt; wrongful restraint and confinement; criminal force and assault; kidnapping, abduction, slavery and forced labour (Sections 100–146).
