Unit I — Foundations

  • Tort = civil wrong, duty primarily fixed by law, usual remedy unliquidated damages.
  • Injuria sine damno → actionable: Ashby v White.
  • Damnum sine injuria → no right violated: Gloucester Grammar School Case.
  • Motive generally irrelevant to lawful act: Bradford Corporation v Pickles.
  • Winfield (general liability) vs Salmond (pigeon-hole theory).

Unit II — Defences & vicarious liability

  • Volenti = knowledge + free voluntary consent; knowledge alone is not consent (Smith v Baker).
  • Act of God = extraordinary natural event; Nichols v Marsland.
  • Inevitable accident despite reasonable care; Stanley v Powell.
  • Private defence must be necessary/proportionate; Bird v Holbrook.
  • Contributory negligence → apportionment; distinguish composite negligence (T.O. Anthony; Khenyei).
  • Employer: course of employment; Limpus (unauthorised mode) vs Joel (frolic).

Unit III — Negligence, nuisance, liability

  • Negligence = duty + breach + causation/damage; Donoghue, Blyth, Barnett.
  • Remoteness: Wagon Mound; thin skull: Smith v Leech Brain.
  • Res ipsa: Byrne v Boadle; Scott v London Docks.
  • Nervous shock: Dulieu, Bourhill, McLoughlin, Alcock.
  • Public nuisance individual claim needs special damage: Rose v Miles.
  • Rylands strict liability (escape/non-natural use) + exceptions; M.C. Mehta absolute liability for hazardous enterprises.

Unit IV — Specific torts

  • Assault = apprehension; battery = contact; false imprisonment = total restraint.
  • Defamation: defamatory statement + reference to plaintiff + publication; defences include truth, honest opinion/fair comment and privilege.
  • Malicious prosecution: prosecution + favourable termination + no reasonable/probable cause + malice + damage.
  • Intimidation: unlawful threat causing loss (Rookes); conspiracy: combination + unlawful means/purpose (Quinn).
  • Property: trespass to land/goods, conversion, detinue; Entick v Carrington.

Unit V — Consumer & motor vehicles (updated law)

  • CPA 2019 (KSLU revision effective 2021–22): Councils, CCPA, Commissions, mediation, product liability, offences/penalties.
  • Jurisdiction: District ≤₹50L; State >₹50L–₹2Cr; National >₹2Cr (consideration paid).
  • CPA limitation generally 2 years (Section 69), condonation possible.
  • MV Act: Section 146 third-party insurance; Section 164 no-fault ₹5L death/₹2.5L grievous hurt; Section 161 hit-and-run Scheme 2022 ₹2L death/₹50K grievous hurt; Section 165 MACT; 166 application; 168 award.
  • Do not use old Section 140 compensation figures or old CPA 1986 forum thresholds as current law.
Exam technique: 2 marks = definition + section/case if applicable. 5 marks = definition + elements + short authority/example. 10/15 marks = introduction, legal test/elements, explanation, case facts/principle, exceptions/defences, application and conclusion. Problem = IRAC.
