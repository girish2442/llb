Unit 1 — General Principles of Crime

Crime and other wrongs; actus reus and mens rea; maxims; mistake, intoxication, compulsion and legally abnormal person; parties to crime; State obligation; history, extent and operation of BNS; definitions and general explanations; gender neutrality; punishments, commutation, fine, default, solitary confinement and general exceptions.

Unit 2 — Inchoate Crimes; Offences Against Women and Children

Abetment (ss.45–60), abetment of offence by person outside India; conspiracy (s.61); attempt (s.62); rape and other sexual offences/deceitful means (ss.63–73); criminal force/assault against women (ss.74–79); dowry death (s.80), bigamy (s.82), cruelty (ss.85–86), kidnapping (s.87); offences against children (ss.91–97).

Detailed notesExam Q&A

Unit 3 — Offences Against the Human Body

Culpable homicide, mob lynching, murder, hit-and-run, causing death by rash/negligent act, suicide-related offences, organised crime, terrorist act; hurt/grievous hurt including permanent disability/vegetative state; wrongful restraint/confinement; force/criminal force/assault; kidnapping, abduction, slavery and forced labour.

Detailed notesExam Q&A

Unit 4 — Offences Against the State, Elections, Public Tranquillity and Public Justice

Offences against State (ss.147–158); election, coin, currency notes and government stamps (ss.168–188); public tranquillity—unlawful assembly, rioting, affray, promoting enmity, prejudicial assertions (ss.189–197); public justice—false/fabricated evidence, destruction of evidence, harbouring offender (ss.227–238); public nuisance (ss.270–273); mischief/criminal trespass (ss.324–334); forgery and property marks (ss.335–344).

Detailed notesExam Q&A

Unit 5 — Offences Against Property and Reputation

Theft and snatching (ss.303–307); extortion (s.308); robbery (s.309 and s.313); dacoity (ss.310–312); criminal misappropriation (ss.314–315); criminal breach of trust (s.316); receiving stolen property (s.317); cheating (ss.318–319); defamation, criminal intimidation, insult, annoyance etc. (ss.351–356).
