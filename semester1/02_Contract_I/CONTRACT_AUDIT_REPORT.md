## Important accuracy and quality observations
- Unit I originally listed counter-offer as a statutory Section 6 mode. Corrected: counter-offer is a case-law consequence of rejection, not a separately enumerated Section 6 mode.
- Unit I needs a clear distinction between Section 4 (completion of communication) and Section 5 (when revocation is permitted); the additional answer now separates them.
- Unit II minor-misrepresentation problem must not suggest that the minor's void contract becomes enforceable. Any restitution is fact-sensitive and subject to statutory limits; the updated wording avoids an automatic recovery claim.
- Unit III coverage of Section 27 restraint of trade is present, but other void-agreement categories and public policy were not adequately represented. Additions now cover Sections 23 and 26–30 at overview level.
- Unit IV Section 74 should not be taught as automatic payment of the named sum. The court awards reasonable compensation up to the stipulated ceiling.
- Unit V needs the post-2018 framework, especially Sections 14, 16 and substituted performance under Section 20. Additions are included; confirm exact statutory wording from the current Act before memorising.
- Some source HTML used Markdown-style **bold** inside HTML. Converted to HTML bold tags where found.
- The original paper-year labels were retained as source claims, not independently certified. Confirm against original KSLU papers before calling a question 'repeated'.
