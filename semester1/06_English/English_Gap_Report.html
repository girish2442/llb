1. Batch and course identification

The student's admission batch is 2024–25. The KSLU English syllabus found for Semester I is Course VI – English (for those who write examinations in Kannada), under the revised three-year LL.B. syllabus introduced for students admitted from 2018–19. The 2024–25 criminal-law syllabus amendment does not itself revise this English course.

Applicability condition: This English course is explicitly labelled for students who write examinations in Kannada. Confirm that the student's college/exam registration places the student in this language-paper option. If the student is registered for a different English paper, do not use this package as the official syllabus.

The syllabus source reproduced on Munotes identifies University Circular 1292 dated 9 August 2018 and reproduces the five units. A college-hosted PDF also reproduces the same unit headings. Syllabus reproduction · College-hosted syllabus PDF.

5. Important limitations / items to confirm

  1. Confirm the student is registered for “English (for those who write examinations in Kannada)”. The syllabus label is a material condition of applicability.
  2. The exact prescribed edition/pages of Gandhi's Law and Lawyers were not uploaded. The Unit I page therefore avoids invented passage details and exact quotations.
  3. The prescribed syllabus lists translation from legal texts and decided cases but does not prescribe a specific case extract in the syllabus excerpt. Exercises here are original practice passages, not official KSLU translation passages.
  4. Question bank questions are model practice, not verified past-paper questions. The package does not label them as PYQs.
  5. Kannada model translations are intentionally not fabricated. Answer checks specify legal meaning that must be preserved; use teacher-approved Kannada legal equivalents.
