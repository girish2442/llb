Recommended study order

  1. Read the syllabus and gap report.
  2. Study the Unit I prescribed chapter itself, then use the reading prompts and questions.
  3. Complete Unit II grammar rules and exercises; check every answer.
  4. Practise one writing task from Unit III and one professional format from Unit IV each day.
  5. Complete translation exercises from Unit V and maintain a glossary.
  6. Use the question bank for timed revision.
