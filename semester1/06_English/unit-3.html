1. Paragraph writing

A paragraph develops one central idea. Use a topic sentence, supporting explanation/examples and a concluding sentence. Avoid unrelated points.

Model paragraph – Importance of legal aid: Legal aid helps people who cannot afford legal services to access justice. It promotes equality before the law by ensuring that poverty does not prevent a person from seeking a remedy. Legal aid may include legal advice, representation and assistance in understanding court procedure. It is especially important for vulnerable persons. An effective legal aid system strengthens public confidence in the justice system.

2. Report writing and press report

A report records facts clearly and objectively. A press report generally has a headline, place/date, lead paragraph answering who/what/when/where, details and a neutral closing. Do not invent facts, quotes or casualty figures.

Model press report – Legal aid awareness camp

LEGAL AID CAMP HELD AT CITY LAW COLLEGE
Hubballi, 12 September: A legal aid awareness camp was organised at City Law College on Thursday to explain free legal services and access to justice. Faculty members and student volunteers explained how eligible persons can approach the Legal Services Authority. A question-and-answer session covered legal assistance, mediation and basic court procedure. The programme concluded with a request to spread awareness among local residents.

3. Précis writing and summarising

A précis is a concise restatement of the central argument of a passage. Read twice, identify the main idea, remove examples/repetition, organise the essential points, write in your own words and give a suitable title. Keep the original meaning and logical order. A summary may be less tightly compressed and may use headings; a précis is usually one coherent passage.

4. Essay writing

Plan: define the topic; state the main position; organise 3–4 body paragraphs; use examples; acknowledge a relevant qualification; conclude with a clear takeaway.

Model essay – Rule of law

The rule of law means that public power must be exercised according to law and not according to the personal wishes of those in authority. It requires equality before the law, fair procedure and accountability. In a constitutional democracy, government officials and ordinary citizens are both subject to law. Independent courts help protect legal rights and review unlawful action. The rule of law also requires laws to be accessible, reasonably clear and applied fairly. It does not mean that every law is automatically just; laws must operate within constitutional limits. A society that respects the rule of law protects liberty, promotes stability and strengthens public trust.

6. Comprehension of unseen passages

Read for the main idea first. Then locate the sentence supporting each answer. Distinguish a fact stated in the passage from an inference. Explain vocabulary from context. Answer only what is asked and avoid importing outside legal knowledge.

7. Letter writing

Formal layout: sender's address; date; receiver's designation/address; subject; salutation; opening purpose; supporting details; requested action; polite close; signature. Use concise paragraphs and a respectful tone.

Model letter – Request for legal aid clinic visit

From: Student Coordinator, Legal Aid Cell, ABC Law College
To: The Secretary, District Legal Services Authority
Date: 10 October 2026
Subject: Request to arrange an awareness session

Sir/Madam,

Our college Legal Aid Cell proposes to conduct an awareness session for students and residents on access to free legal services. We request your office to nominate a resource person and suggest a suitable date. We will arrange the venue and inform participants.

We would be grateful for your assistance.

Yours faithfully,
Student Coordinator

Practice tasks

  1. Write a 120-word paragraph on the importance of legal literacy.
  2. Prepare a press report on a moot court competition held at your college. Use only the facts given by your teacher; if facts are not supplied, label assumptions clearly.
  3. Write a précis of the passage below in about one-third of its length.
  4. Write an essay: “Technology and access to justice”.
  5. Join the sentences using a suitable cohesive device: “The accused was absent. The court adjourned the matter.”
  6. Write a formal letter requesting a legal aid awareness session.
Précis passage: Courts resolve disputes and protect rights, but many people do not understand how to approach them. Legal literacy helps citizens know their rights, duties and available remedies. Schools, colleges, legal services authorities and community organisations can spread this knowledge. Awareness must be practical: people should learn where to seek advice, how to preserve documents and why deadlines matter. A legally informed society is better able to prevent disputes and use lawful remedies when disputes arise.

Model answers / marking guide

Précis title: Legal Literacy and Access to Justice.

Model précis: Legal literacy enables people to understand their rights, duties and remedies. Educational institutions, legal services authorities and community groups can teach practical steps such as seeking advice, preserving documents and observing deadlines. An informed public can prevent disputes and use lawful remedies effectively.

Cohesive sentence: The accused was absent; therefore, the court adjourned the matter.

Report / essay / letter assessment: Award marks for correct format, relevance, logical organisation, grammar, factual accuracy and concise expression. Do not invent facts in reports.

Quick revision

  • Paragraph = one controlling idea.
  • Report = factual and neutral.
  • Précis = shorter, own words, central ideas only.
  • Essay = introduction, organised body, conclusion.
  • Cohesive devices show logical relationships; do not overuse them.
  • Comprehension answers must be supported by the passage.
  • Formal letters need a clear subject and specific request.
