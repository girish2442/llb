Common error checks

  • Each/every/either/neither + singular verb: Each party is entitled…
  • One of + plural noun + singular verb: One of the witnesses was present.
  • Senior/junior/superior/inferior + to.
  • Accused of; comply with; interested in; responsible for.
  • Use parallel forms: to read, to write and to argue.
  • Distinguish advice (noun) / advise (verb); principal / principle; council / counsel.

Voice

Active: The court issued the notice. Passive: The notice was issued by the court. Present perfect: The court has issued the notice → The notice has been issued by the court. Modal: The court may grant relief → Relief may be granted by the court.

Universal truths generally remain in the present tense. Questions use asked/enquired and appropriate word order. Commands use told/ordered/requested + object + to-infinitive.

Cohesive and legal devices

Addition: moreover, furthermore. Contrast: however, nevertheless. Cause: because, since. Result: therefore, consequently. Sequence: first, next, finally. Qualification: subject to, provided that. Override: notwithstanding. Clarification: for the avoidance of doubt. Conclusion: in conclusion.
